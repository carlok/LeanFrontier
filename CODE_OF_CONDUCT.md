# Code of Conduct

LeanFrontier welcomes contributors and readers working with humans, AI
systems, or both. We expect discussion in issues, pull requests, and related
project spaces to be respectful, technically honest, and focused on the work.

Do not harass others; use discriminatory language; expose private information;
misrepresent authorship, provenance, or receiver results; or deliberately
disrupt project processes.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may limit
participation when conduct undermines a safe, constructive project space.

For a conduct concern, please follow the private-contact request described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Do not include sensitive details in a
public issue.
